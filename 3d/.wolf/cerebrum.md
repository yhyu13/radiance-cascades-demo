- [2026-05-24] **Spatial trilinear (`useSpatialTrilinear=1`) on this scene is a NET INTEGRATION-QUALITY LOSER AND a cam0/cam2 SYMMETRIZER simultaneously.** (h.c)' A/B at MB-OFF b=2 M0 measured: ST=1 cam0/cam2 ratios = 0.4348/0.2825; ST=0 ratios = 0.5271/0.3071. BOTH cams brighten under nearest-parent (cam0 +21%, cam2 +9%), getting closer to PT — so ST=1 was diluting both cams by averaging in dim neighbor probes. AT THE SAME TIME, the spread cam2/cam0 widens (0.65 → 0.58) under ST=0 because cam0 brightens 3.75× more than cam2 (cam0's near-uniform fract distribution lets it benefit fully from picking a single bright probe; cam2's fract-corner-bias means cam2 was already effectively at nearest-parent under ST=1 — see h.c §3). **Two architectural implications**: (1) spatial trilinear on a scene with significant probe-content variance can REGRESS quality, opposite of its stated purpose — for any probe-based GI scheme, validate spatial smoothing reduces RMSE vs PT before defaulting it ON; the "averaging always helps" intuition is wrong when neighbors are independently informed about distinct geometry. (2) Spatial smoothing being a symmetrizer rather than a contributor reveals that the cam0/cam2 spread asymmetry source is UPSTREAM of spatial weighting — in the per-direction-bin atlas content at the cam2-visible probes themselves (bake-side). The (h.b)/(h.c)/(h.c)' chain rules out the entire DOWNSTREAM consumption path of the cascade; pivot to bake-side direction-bin gathering. **Rule when an A/B flips a "smoothing" feature**: if both arms move in the SAME direction (both brighter or both dimmer) but unequally, the feature is acting as a symmetrizer not a contributor; the underlying asymmetry source is BEFORE the smoothing step. Use per-cam absolute deltas (not just delta-of-deltas) to disambiguate. See [doc/7/v20_cprime_spatial_trilinear_impl.md](../doc/7/v20_cprime_spatial_trilinear_impl.md).
- [2026-05-24] **Pre-committed 1D verdict bands on a DISTRIBUTION COMPARISON catch the wrong feature — must include a shape-asymmetry sub-test.** (h.c) probe-cell fract viz pre-committed bands on `mean_d` (mean distance from probe-cell center). cam0 vs cam2 delta landed at +0.0303, inside [-0.05, +0.05] NEUTRAL — would have produced a clean "hypothesis rejected" if I'd stopped at the verdict. BUT the histogram revealed cam2 piles 47.7% of pixels into [0.40, 0.45) (vs cam0's 19.6% — a 2.44× concentration), and cam2 mean_fract is 3.9× farther from cell center than cam0's, biased to a specific (low-Y + low-Z) corner. The mean was symmetric only because cam0's broader distribution and cam2's narrower-but-shifted distribution happened to integrate near the same expected value. Third instance of the cerebrum DNR pattern (single-axis summary missing 2D signal — earlier: mode-18 brightness avg masking GI composition, MIXED-boundary verdict). **Rule:** when the experiment is "compare two distributions" (not "measure one scalar"), the pre-committed verdict MUST include at least one shape-asymmetry sub-test alongside the mean: KL-divergence, Earth-mover distance, χ² over histogram bins, OR an explicit per-bin ratio band ("flag if any bin ratio > 2.0×"). The 1D mean is a summary; a summary can be symmetric while the underlying shape is wildly asymmetric. The histogram print in the analyzer narrowly saved the (h.c) interpretation; future analyzers should formalize this rather than rely on visual inspection of the printed table.

## Decision Log

- **Non-co-located probes (Phase 5d):** Chosen over co-located for better GI accuracy at cascade boundaries; trilinear upper-cascade lookup needed as a result
- **Two-script RenderDoc architecture (Phase 6b):** qrenderdoc embeds Python 3.6 which can't use modern `anthropic` SDK. Solution: rdoc_extract.py runs in qrenderdoc, analyze_renderdoc.py runs separately with regular Python
- **Probe jitter excluded from default (Phase 9):** Full cascade rebuild per jittered frame too expensive at 32³ probes × 4 cascades
- **Bilateral blur over tricubic (Phase 9d):** Tricubic needs 64 fetches vs 8; blur achieves similar smoothing at a fraction of the cost
- **Analytic SDF over mesh SDF (Phase 0/7):** Toggle exists; analytic is default for speed + correctness; mesh SDF from `sdf_3d.comp` is optional
