## 2. Banding in Mode 6

The dark "comb" / "venetian-blind" striping on the floor, ceiling, and lower walls is **not** a GI probe artifact — it has the wrong character for that:

- **Shape**: thin vertical slats on the occluder blocks, and concentric arc-like rings on the ceiling/floor near the camera.
- **Spacing**: sub-pixel-aligned, identical across all three modes (mode 0, 3, **and** 6).
- **Diagnosis**: this is a **raymarch step / sample-count artifact** in the primary visibility pass, not a probe-grid or directional-bin issue. Because it appears identically in the final composite and the indirect-only pass, it is upstream of GI. The `raymarchTimeMs: 0.12` is suspiciously low — step count is likely too small for the volume.

True probe-grid banding would appear as **axis-aligned blocky patches at ~½ probe spacing** (≈volumeSize/baseRes = 4/32 = 0.125 units). I do not see that signature in mode 6 — the GI itself looks smooth where it is sampled.
